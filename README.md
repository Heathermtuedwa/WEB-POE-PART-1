## Project Overview

Amara & Co. is a fictional South African clothing retail business offering everyday clothing and accessories. This repository contains the **Part 1 submission** of the WEDE5020 Proof of Evidence: the foundational five-page HTML structure of the website.

At this stage the website contains semantic HTML content only. No external stylesheet or JavaScript has been added yet — visual styling is introduced in Part 2, and interactivity, SEO and forms functionality is introduced in Part 3.

The website consists of five main pages:

* Home
* About Us
* Shop
* Enquiry
* Contact

---

## Target Audience

The target audience consists of South African consumers interested in everyday clothing and accessories.

The website is designed for users who value:

* Comfortable everyday clothing.
* Practical and versatile products.
* Clear product information.
* Simple navigation.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

The primary goals and objectives of the website are to:

* Present Amara & Co. as a professional clothing retailer.
* Provide information about the organisation, its mission and its team.
* Display clothing and accessories clearly, organised by category.
* Provide an accessible enquiry process.
* Allow visitors to contact the organisation, including via more than one location.
* Provide clear internal navigation between all pages.

### Key Performance Indicators

Website success can be measured through:

* Successful navigation between all five pages.
* Enquiry form submissions (functionality added in Part 3).
* Contact form submissions (functionality added in Part 3).
* Search engine visibility (SEO added in Part 3).

## Key Features and Functionality (Part 1 Scope)

* Five interconnected HTML pages.
* Consistent navigation menu across all pages.
* Semantic HTML5 structure (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `footer`, `section`, `article`).

## Website Pages

### Home — `index.html`

The homepage introduces Amara & Co. and provides visitors with an overview of the organisation and navigation to the main areas of the website.

### About Us — `about.html`

The About Us page provides information about the organisation, including its background, mission, vision, and the team behind it.

### Shop — `products.html`

The Shop page displays the available products.

**Womenswear**

* Linen Wrap Dress
* Cotton Poplin Shirt
* Tailored Wide-Leg Trousers

**Menswear**

* Everyday Crew Tee
* Cotton Overshirt
* Straight-Fit Chinos

**Accessories**

* Canvas Tote Bag
* Woven Belt

A size guide table is also included. Product search and the image lightbox are added in Part 3.

### Enquiry — `enquiry.html`

The Enquiry page contains a form that allows visitors to submit product and service enquiries. Validation and processing are added in Part 3.

### Contact — `contact.html`

The Contact page provides contact information, a map covering the Cape Town showroom and the Johannesburg collection point, and a general contact form. The map becomes interactive and the form gains validation/email processing in Part 3.
