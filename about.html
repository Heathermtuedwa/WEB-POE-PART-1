Our Story

Amara & Co. launched in 2021 out of frustration with fast fashion — clothes that looked good for one season and fell apart the next. Our founder, a Cape Town-based designer, set out to build a small online store around a different idea: fewer, better pieces, made from fabrics that last and cuts that don't go out of style.

What started as a small capsule collection sold directly through Instagram has grown into a full online store, still run by a small team who personally check every fabric batch before it becomes a garment.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To offer well-made, versatile clothing at a fair price, so customers can build a wardrobe that lasts instead of one that's replaced every season.

Our Vision

A more sustainable, slower approach to fashion becoming the norm for everyday South African shoppers, not just a niche choice.

Who We Dress

Our customers are people who want their wardrobe to work harder — professionals, students, and everyone in between who wants clothing that fits well, feels good, and doesn't need replacing every few months. We ship nationwide and are based online-first, with a small Cape Town showroom for local customers who want to see and feel the fabrics in person.
